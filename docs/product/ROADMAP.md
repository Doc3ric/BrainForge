# BrainForge — Product Roadmap

> **Document Type:** Product Documentation
> **Location:** `docs/product/ROADMAP.md`
> **Version:** 0.1.0
> **Status:** Frozen

The natural progression of BrainForge focuses on evolving from a structured language learning platform into an AI English tutor.

## Roadmap Phases

1. **Phase 1: Foundation**
   - Core API and Web application scaffolding.
   - Initial database schemas.
2. **Phase 2: Authentication**
   - User registration and login.
   - Protected routes and persistent sessions.
3. **Phase 3: Gamification**
   - Shared gamification infrastructure.
   - XP, Streaks, Daily Goals, and Achievements.
4. **Phase 4: Vocabulary** *(Completed)*
   - Spaced Repetition (SM-2) engine.
   - Vocabulary learning and review sessions.
5. **Phase 5: Conversation Practice** *(Next)*
   - AI chat interface with streaming responses.
   - Conversation scenarios (Travel, Interview, Business, etc.).
   - Grammar feedback and Vocabulary highlights during chat.
6. **Phase 6: Grammar Checker**
   - AI-powered free-text grammar correction and practice.
7. **Phase 7: Writing Coach**
   - Structured writing prompts and essay evaluation.
8. **Phase 8: Speaking & Pronunciation**
   - Voice-to-text input, pronunciation scoring, and audio feedback.
9. **Phase 9: Interview Simulator**
   - Real-time spoken interactions mimicking IELTS or Job Interviews.
10. **Phase 10: Final Polish**
    - UI/UX refinements, predictive analytics, and performance optimization.
